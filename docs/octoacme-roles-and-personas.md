# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure that features are user-centered, intuitive, and accessible. They conduct research, create prototypes, and validate designs with users before development.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Define information architecture and user flows
- Collaborate with Product Managers on feature requirements
- Ensure accessibility and design consistency
- Iterate based on user feedback and analytics

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, delightful user experiences
- Reduce design-to-development rework
- Increase user satisfaction and product adoption

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Partner on feature prioritization and acceptance criteria; validate requirements through user research
- **Developers**: Collaborate on technical feasibility; provide detailed design specs and assets
- **Data Analysts**: Review user behavior data to inform design decisions
- **QA/Testing**: Ensure design acceptance criteria are testable and clear

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions
- Prototype walkthroughs and user testing reports
- Design system and component documentation

---

## Data Analysts

### Role Summary
Data Analysts deliver actionable insights from project and product metrics. They identify trends, flag risks, and help teams make evidence-based decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) and success metrics
- Analyze user behavior, adoption, and engagement data
- Identify performance bottlenecks and opportunities for improvement
- Create dashboards and reports for stakeholders
- Support A/B testing and experimentation
- Highlight risks or anomalies in product health

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision making
- Measure impact of features and initiatives
- Identify and mitigate product risks early

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share insights on user behavior and feature impact; support prioritization decisions
- **Project Managers**: Provide project health metrics and risk signals
- **UX/UI Designers**: Share user behavior data to inform design iterations
- **Developers**: Collaborate on instrumentation and observability requirements

### Typical Communication
- Weekly metric reviews and dashboards
- Data-driven recommendations and trend reports
- Experimentation planning and results analysis

---

## Customer Support Lead

### Role Summary
Customer Support Leads act as the voice of the customer. They gather feedback from support interactions, identify patterns, and escalate issues that impact customer experience.

### Responsibilities
- Manage escalations and high-impact customer issues
- Identify recurring support patterns and root causes
- Communicate customer feedback to Product and Engineering teams
- Collaborate on documentation and self-service improvements
- Track customer satisfaction and support metrics
- Provide early warning on product usability or quality issues

### Goals
- Maximize customer satisfaction and retention
- Reduce support volume through self-service and product improvements
- Surface customer insights to drive product decisions

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Share customer feedback and pain points to inform prioritization
- **Project Managers**: Escalate critical customer issues and timeline impacts
- **Developers**: Collaborate on bug reports and technical root causes
- **QA/Testing**: Provide real-world user scenarios for testing

### Typical Communication
- Weekly customer feedback summaries
- Escalation reports and issue tracking
- Documentation and FAQ updates

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify requirements, translate business needs into actionable work, and bridge communication between stakeholders and delivery teams. They ensure alignment between business goals and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Translate business needs into technical specifications
- Clarify ambiguities and scope with stakeholders
- Define acceptance criteria in collaboration with Product and QA
- Document decision rationale and trade-offs
- Monitor scope creep and flag requirement changes

### Goals
- Ensure shared understanding across all teams
- Reduce rework due to requirement misalignment
- Maintain clear, traceable documentation

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Validate business priorities and success metrics
- **Project Managers**: Support scope management and timeline planning
- **Developers**: Clarify technical requirements and feasibility
- **QA/Testing**: Work with QA to define testable acceptance criteria

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documentation and specs
- Clarification meetings and decision logs
- Acceptance criteria and scope change requests

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
