# OctoAcme — Role Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure new team members quickly understand their responsibilities, interactions, and project context.

## Pre-Onboarding (Before First Day)

### For the Hiring Manager/PM
- [ ] Ensure new team member has access to GitHub repository
- [ ] Add to project board and communication channels (Slack, email lists)
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 with new team member
- [ ] Prepare project overview materials
- [ ] Assign a buddy/mentor from the team

## First Day

### New Team Member Checklist
- [ ] Review project charter and one-pager
- [ ] Read OctoAcme Project Management Overview
- [ ] Review your specific role description in octoacme-roles-and-personas.md
- [ ] Set up dev environment (if applicable)
- [ ] Join team communication channels
- [ ] Attend daily standup
- [ ] Meet your buddy/mentor

## First Week

### Learning & Context
- [ ] **Project Understanding**
  - [ ] Attend project kickoff or overview meeting
  - [ ] Review project timeline and milestones
  - [ ] Understand success metrics and KPIs
  - [ ] Review risk register and known blockers

- [ ] **Role-Specific Onboarding** (See role-specific sections below)
  - [ ] Understand your responsibilities and decision rights
  - [ ] Meet key collaborators from other roles
  - [ ] Review your role's communication patterns
  - [ ] Understand escalation paths

- [ ] **Process Documentation**
  - [ ] Review octoacme-project-planning.md
  - [ ] Review octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md
  - [ ] Review octoacme-roles-interaction-guide.md
  - [ ] Understand team's Definition of Done

### First Meetings
- [ ] Daily standup with delivery team
- [ ] 1:1 with your manager
- [ ] 1:1 with PM or Product Manager
- [ ] Meet your buddy
- [ ] (If remote) intro meeting with broader team

## First Month

### Deliverables & Contribution
- [ ] Complete first assigned task or story
- [ ] Participate in sprint planning
- [ ] Attend sprint review/demo
- [ ] Attend retrospective
- [ ] Provide feedback on onboarding experience

### Additional Context
- [ ] Review past retrospective notes and action items
- [ ] Understand current blockers and dependencies
- [ ] Review recent releases and decisions
- [ ] Establish regular 1:1 cadence with manager

## Role-Specific Onboarding

### Product Manager Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review product roadmap and prioritization framework
- [ ] Understand success metrics and how they're tracked
- [ ] Review customer research and feedback sources
- [ ] Meet key stakeholders and understand their priorities
- [ ] Review backlog prioritization criteria
- [ ] Understand definition of acceptance criteria
- [ ] Schedule stakeholder intro meetings

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- PM (Project Manager)
- Data Analyst (for metrics and insights)
- Support Lead (for customer feedback)
- Senior Developer (for technical feasibility discussions)

---

### Project Manager Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review project timeline, milestones, and critical path
- [ ] Understand budget and resource constraints (if applicable)
- [ ] Review risk register and current mitigation plans
- [ ] Understand team velocity and capacity
- [ ] Review previous project retrospectives
- [ ] Understand escalation matrix and stakeholder communication plan
- [ ] Set up project dashboards and tracking

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Product Manager (for priorities and roadmap)
- Tech Lead (for technical timeline estimates)
- QA Lead (for quality and testing timeline)
- Stakeholder representatives

---

### Developer Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Set up dev environment and verify build passes
- [ ] Review coding standards and PR process
- [ ] Understand CI/CD pipeline and deployment process
- [ ] Review existing architecture and design documents
- [ ] Understand test requirements and coverage expectations
- [ ] Review security and accessibility requirements
- [ ] Complete first PR and code review

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Tech Lead (for technical guidance)
- UX/UI Designer (for design specifications)
- QA (for testing expectations)
- Business Analyst (for requirement clarification)

---

### UX/UI Designer Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review design system and component library
- [ ] Understand accessibility requirements and standards
- [ ] Review user research and personas
- [ ] Understand design review and approval process
- [ ] Review current product designs and wireframes
- [ ] Schedule design critique and feedback sessions
- [ ] Understand user testing process

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Product Manager (for feature requirements and priorities)
- Senior Developer (for technical feasibility)
- UX Research Lead (if applicable)
- QA (for testability of designs)

---

### Data Analyst Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review current KPIs and success metrics
- [ ] Understand data sources and instrumentation
- [ ] Access to analytics tools and dashboards
- [ ] Review past metric reports and trends
- [ ] Understand experimentation framework
- [ ] Learn stakeholder reporting needs
- [ ] Review data privacy and compliance requirements

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Product Manager (for business goals and metrics)
- PM (for project health metrics)
- Developers (for instrumentation requirements)
- Support Lead (for customer health signals)

---

### Customer Support Lead Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review support ticketing system and processes
- [ ] Understand product features and common issues
- [ ] Review customer feedback and common pain points
- [ ] Understand SLA and escalation procedures
- [ ] Meet support team members
- [ ] Review recent customer issues and resolutions
- [ ] Understand product roadmap and planned improvements

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Product Manager (for roadmap and feature decisions)
- Support team members
- QA (for quality issues)
- Developers (for technical troubleshooting)

---

### Business Analyst Onboarding

**Additional Focus Areas:**
- [ ] Review project requirements and scope document
- [ ] Understand stakeholder needs and constraints
- [ ] Review acceptance criteria standards
- [ ] Understand traceability and documentation requirements
- [ ] Review decision logs from past projects
- [ ] Understand change management process
- [ ] Meet key stakeholders and business leads

**Key Collaborators to Meet:**
- Product Manager (for business priorities)
- PM (for schedule and constraints)
- Developers (for technical feasibility discussions)
- QA (for testing approach)

## 30-60-90 Day Expectations

### Days 1-30: Learning & Context
- Complete all onboarding tasks
- Contribute to 2-3 small tasks
- Understand project goals and your role
- Build relationships with key collaborators
- **Success Metric**: Able to articulate project vision and your role independently

### Days 30-60: Contribution & Independence
- Lead or own 1-2 sprint stories
- Provide feedback on processes or documentation
- Identify areas for improvement
- Increase complexity and ownership of work
- **Success Metric**: Delivering work with minimal supervision

### Days 60-90: Ownership & Impact
- Own a major feature or initiative
- Mentor or assist other new team members
- Contribute to process improvements
- Prepare for first retrospective participation
- **Success Metric**: Independently leading work that impacts project goals

## Post-Onboarding

### 90-Day Check-In
- [ ] Formal 1:1 review with manager
- [ ] Share feedback on onboarding experience
- [ ] Adjust expectations and goals for next quarter
- [ ] Identify areas for continued learning
- [ ] Celebrate progress and contributions

### Continuous Learning
- [ ] Attend monthly process improvement discussions
- [ ] Participate in knowledge-sharing sessions
- [ ] Review and contribute to documentation updates
- [ ] Mentor new team members as they join
